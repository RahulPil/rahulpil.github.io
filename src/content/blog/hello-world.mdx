This is the third time I've started a personal site. The first two times I picked a
template, filled in some placeholder text, and abandoned the project after a week. I am
hoping the third time is different. I am also hoping I am wrong about my own track record.

## What this is for

Three things:

1. **Notes** — derivations, intuitions, and reading summaries from the parts of machine
   learning I'm currently sitting with. The audience is mostly me, six months from now.
2. **Projects** — the artifacts of trying to understand something by building it.
3. **Writing** — occasional longer essays, when something is interesting enough to merit
   thinking about in public.

## Rules

A few constraints I'm setting on myself, because past me has been bad at this.

- **Publish before it's done.** Half-finished notes are still useful to me; perfect ones
  rarely materialize.
- **Cite generously.** Most of what I'll write is downstream of someone else's idea.
- **Be wrong publicly.** If I revise my opinion, leave the old version visible.
- **No analytics.** I don't actually want to know who is reading.

If any of this turns out to be wrong, future posts will admit so.
