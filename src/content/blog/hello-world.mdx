I've been telling myself I'd put up a personal site for about three years. The
delay was mostly the usual one: I kept waiting until I had something worth
publishing, and the bar for "worth publishing" kept inching up. The fix, in
retrospect, is the obvious one — lower the bar, ship the scaffolding, and let
the content catch up later.

So: this is the scaffolding. The plan is to use this space for two things.
**Notes** are short, technical, and mostly written for me six months from now —
the kind of thing I'd otherwise lose in a notebook. **Writing** is for longer
posts: reading summaries, half-formed opinions, occasional retrospectives on
research and engineering.

I won't promise a cadence. I will promise that nothing here is final — if you
catch me being wrong about something, please tell me.
